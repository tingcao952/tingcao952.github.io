excerpt: 'Deploying large language models on resource-constrained mobile devices remains challenging because of memory limitations and high I/O overhead. DWI combines end-to-end dynamic width selection on nested LLMs, a cyclic-locality-aware cache policy for evicting stale parameters, and a block-wise load-computation pipeline that overlaps in-memory computation with loading from flash. Experiments show that DWI accelerates on-device LLM inference by up to 34x while maintaining competitive model performance.'
