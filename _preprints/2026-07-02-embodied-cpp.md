excerpt: 'Deploying embodied AI models remains fragmented across model-specific Python stacks, backend assumptions, and robot-side code. Embodied.cpp is a portable C++ runtime that captures a shared execution path for vision-language-action and world-action models through input adapters, sequence builders, backbone execution, head plugins, and deployment adapters. It supports multi-rate execution, latency-first fused inference, and extensible operators and interfaces across heterogeneous devices, robots, and simulators. Across three VLA and two WAM models, it achieves 1.05–2.70x speedups and 7–77% lower VRAM than Python baselines while preserving control quality.'
