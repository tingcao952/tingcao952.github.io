excerpt: 'Visual-token pruning is a discrete, non-convex problem for which continuous-gradient approximations often produce suboptimal solutions under aggressive compression. GRIP-VLM formulates pruning as a Markov Decision Process and uses Group Relative Policy Optimization with supervised warm-up to explore the discrete selection space. Its budget-aware scorer dynamically estimates per-token importance and adapts to arbitrary compression ratios without retraining. Across multimodal benchmarks, GRIP-VLM outperforms heuristic and supervised baselines and delivers up to 15% faster inference at equal accuracy.'
